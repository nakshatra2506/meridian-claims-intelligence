## Retrieval scope

These documents answer **conceptual** questions only.

- Counts, totals, averages, rankings and comparisons come from the structured
  data layer, never from vector similarity.
- Risk scores, levels, factors and anomalies come from the risk engine, and are
  retrieved rather than calculated.
